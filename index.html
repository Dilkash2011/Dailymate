About DailyMate

DailyMate is a free online collection of practical tools designed to make everyday calculations and simple digital tasks easier.

The website brings commonly needed tools such as EMI, GST, discount, SIP, percentage, age and BMI calculators together in one convenient place.

DailyMate also includes text utilities such as a word counter, case converter and password generator.

Who can use DailyMate?

DailyMate is designed for students, working professionals, small business users and anyone who needs a quick calculation or simple online utility.

Our goal

Our goal is to provide simple, easy-to-understand and accessible digital tools that can be used on computers, tablets and mobile phones.

Understanding DailyMate Calculators

EMI Calculator

An EMI calculator estimates the fixed monthly payment for a loan based on principal amount, interest rate and tenure. Actual loan payments may differ because of lender-specific charges, rates and conditions.

GST Calculator

A GST calculator helps estimate Goods and Services Tax on a given amount. Actual tax treatment depends on the applicable Indian tax rules and transaction details.

SIP Calculator

A SIP calculator estimates the potential value of regular investments using an assumed rate of return. Market-linked investments can go up or down, and calculator results are not guaranteed returns.

BMI Calculator

BMI is a general screening measure based on height and weight. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and should not replace professional medical advice.

Percentage Calculator

Percentage calculations are useful for marks, discounts, business calculations, statistics and many everyday mathematical tasks.
